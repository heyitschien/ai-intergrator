# Real Examples — three stock images (what & why)

These **Unsplash** photos are wired in `src/components/RealExamples.tsx` so you get real visuals without producing custom art. Swap URLs anytime.

| Card | Who it represents | What the photo should communicate |
|------|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Individuals & everyday life** | Students, seniors, parents, anyone learning AI for daily life | Calm focus with a laptop—approachable, professional, not “stock hacker” cliché |
| **Small businesses & local teams** | Owners, front desks, light ops | Real customer or counter moment—trust, locality, practical service |
| **Community & shared learning** | Schools, nonprofits, neighbors learning together | Small group, friendly collaboration—human pace, not a conference hall |
