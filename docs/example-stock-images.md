# Use case photography (Unsplash)

Stock images are wired **per use case** in `src/components/UseCases.tsx` (four cards). They add a human, “who this is for” signal **without** a separate “Who this is for?” section—same narrative, less repetition.

| # | Scenario | Image intent |
|---|----------|----------------|
| **01** | Design / visualize for clients | Interior or space that reads as design-led and visual |
| **02** | Small business workflow | Real service or counter moment—local, operational |
| **03** | Individuals learning AI | Calm focus with a laptop—approachable, not “tech bro” |
| **04** | Creators → systems | Small team collaborating—workflows and creative ops |
