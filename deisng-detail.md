I mapped out the full blueprint in a build-ready format.

The core direction is: **calm, modern, human-centered tech**. Not a consultant sales page, not a résumé, and not a flashy AI landing page. It should feel like a clear introduction to how you think and how you help.

A few key decisions I’d stand by:

**Color system**
Use a soft near-white background, deep slate/navy text, and one restrained blue accent. That combination signals trust, clarity, and technology without feeling cold. It also matches your personality better than neon or dark cyber aesthetics.

**Typography**
Inter is the safest strong choice because it reads beautifully on mobile and feels modern without being trendy. If you want slightly more personality, use Sora or Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and keep Inter for body.

**Structure**
The page should answer 5 questions fast:
Who are you?
What do you do?
Who do you help?
How do you think?
How can someone reach out?

That’s why the architecture is:
Hero → What I Help With → Who I Help → How I Think About AI → Example Use Cases → Story → Connect.

**Psychology**
A new visitor should feel:
“This is clear.”
“This is relevant to me.”
“This person seems thoughtful and grounded.”
“This is someone I’d feel comfortable talking to.”

That means the copy should stay warm, plainspoken, and practical.
